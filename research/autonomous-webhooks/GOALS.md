# Goals (Jordan, 2026-09-08)

Target about **$10k/mo** — real side business, not venture scale, not “a couple grand forever.”

## Go-to-market shape

**More people, less money per seat** — volume / low ARPU, not a handful of enterprise security contracts.

Implications vs earlier research passes:

- Do **not** optimize for SOC 2 / Lakera-class buyers / acquisition narratives by default
- Prefer simple paid plans many self-hosted agent operators can buy
- Product stays: hardened wake on **customer-owned** compute (Herdr-like); we don’t run workloads
- Hosted ingress can stay rented (Svix/Hookdeck) while the on-box connector + policy is what we sell
